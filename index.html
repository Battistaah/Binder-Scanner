Artwork recognition

The scanner learns the pictures of every set you track, so it can recognise cards by their artwork as well as their printed number. This is what makes full-art cards, old cards and binder pages work. Each set downloads 3–8 MB once.

Scanning tips

Single cards: fill the outline with the card. Even light, no glare on the bottom strip: that's where the number and set code live. Keep the card still for about a second.

Binder pages: track the binder's set first (Find → Sets) so every pocket can be matched by its artwork. Hold the phone flat over the page, fit the pockets to the grid, and avoid ceiling-light glare on the sleeves. A photo taken straight on and cropped to the page also works.
